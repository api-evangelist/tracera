# Tracera

> Tracera (formerly ESG Flo) is an AI-powered corporate sustainability / ESG data platform that automates the collection, verification, auditing, and reporting of sustainability data with finance-grade accuracy and traceability. It extracts audit-ready metrics from raw data across PDFs, spreadsheets, and ERP systems for 900+ metrics, supports frameworks like CSRD, SEC, GRI, and CDP, and serves enterprise customers across manufacturing, real estate, construction, retail, technology, and healthcare. Founded in 2022 (launched 2023), New York based, $12M Series A (April 2025) led by Foundry with Rho Ignition, Tola Capital, and Contour Venture Partners; $17.25M raised in total. No public developer API, developer portal, or SDKs were found as of this profile.
